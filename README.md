# StudyCycle

**WARNING: StudyCycle is in a very experimental phase — expect breaking changes, incomplete features, and potential data loss.**

## About

StudyCycle is a **federated platform** for students to share and collaborate on study material. It is built for academic communities while running across a decentralized network of instances.

## History

StudyCycle is a **hard fork** of [Lemmy](https://github.com/LemmyNet/lemmy). It diverges from Lemmy to focus specifically on the needs of students and academic collaboration.

## Current Phase

StudyCycle is in early development. The API, database schema, and federation behavior may change significantly and without notice.
